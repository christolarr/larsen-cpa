Who This Is For

Owner-operated businesses, professional practices, restaurants and retailers, real estate investors, and other small businesses that want their books kept current and ready for tax time.

Bookkeeping Services Provided

  1. Transaction Coding and Reconciliations

    Every transaction coded, with bank and credit card accounts reconciled to their statements.

  2. Monthly or Quarterly Close

    A reliable close on the schedule that suits the business.

  3. Financial Statements

    Income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement prepared from your records.

  4. QuickBooks and Xero Setup and Cleanup

    Implementation of a new file, or remediation of an existing one that has fallen behind.

  5. Payroll, Sales Tax, and 1099 Coordination

    Coordination with your payroll provider, sales tax filings, and year-end Forms 1099.

  6. Business Tax Returns

    Federal and California returns prepared from the same books at year end. More on small business accounting.

How It Works

  1. 01

    Enquiry and Records Review

    A short enquiry and a look at your current records and prior year return.

  2. 02

    Written Scope and Fee

    The scope and fee are confirmed in writing before work begins.

  3. 03

    Onboarding of Accounts and Records

    Accounts, statements, and accounting files are brought in and organized.

  4. 04

    Close and Returns

    Monthly or quarterly close, with returns prepared at year end.

To Begin

Please provide recent bank and credit card statements, the prior year tax return, and your current accounting file if you have one. The scope and fee are confirmed in writing before work begins. You can also email connect@larsen-cpa.com.
